# Steering Notes

## Focus
Residual-stream concept-direction interventions and their interpretability limits.

## Key Notes by Source ID
1. `S1 (ActAdd)`:
- Demonstrates behavior steering through activation differences from contrastive prompts.
- Supports non-parametric intervention paradigm for this thesis.

2. `S2 (ITI)`:
- Uses inference-time linear interventions to improve truthfulness.
- Indicates actionable causal signal in activations without retraining.

3. `S3/S4/S5 (Refusal direction line)`:
- S3 and S4 support strong directional mediation.
- S5 challenges over-simplified single-direction framing; indicates multi-component structure.

4. `S6/S7 (Dynamic steering)`:
- Context-aware and composed vectors may outperform static vectors.
- Suggests this thesis should include context-conditional controls.

5. `S8/S9 (Identifiability and decomposition caveats)`:
- Steering vectors may not uniquely map to mechanistic causes.
- Post-hoc decomposition (SAE-based) can help but is not definitive.

## Operational Outcomes
1. Include at least two vector estimation methods.
2. Include context-shift prompts to test brittleness of static vectors.
3. Avoid overclaiming causal uniqueness from one direction.
