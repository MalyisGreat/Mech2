# Master Task Board

## Coordination Model
Each workstream is treated as a subagent-style lane with independent goals, logs, and outputs coordinated through this board.

## Workstreams

1. `WS1 - Conceptual Framing`
- Goal: sharpen definitions of identity, drift, recovery, rebound.
- Output: `research/syntheses/ws1_conceptual_framing.md`
- Status: completed

2. `WS2 - Activation Steering & Residual Interventions`
- Goal: collect primary methods and known effects of residual-stream injections.
- Output: `research/syntheses/ws2_activation_steering.md`
- Status: completed

3. `WS3 - Scaling and Internal Stability Signals`
- Goal: gather evidence on how scale changes internal representations and robustness.
- Output: `research/syntheses/ws3_scaling_stability.md`
- Status: completed

4. `WS4 - Fine-Tuning Effects (Full FT vs LoRA)`
- Goal: determine how adaptation methods alter steerability and identity retention.
- Output: `research/syntheses/ws4_finetuning_effects.md`
- Status: completed

5. `WS5 - Experimental Design Blueprint`
- Goal: specify models, datasets/prompts, interventions, and controls.
- Output: `experiments/experimental_blueprint.md`
- Status: completed

6. `WS6 - Metrics, Statistics, and Causal Tests`
- Goal: formalize drift/recovery metrics and tests for active stabilization vs dilution.
- Output: `experiments/metrics_and_stats.md`
- Status: completed

7. `WS7 - Risk, Validity, and Reproducibility`
- Goal: identify confounders, threats, compute limits, and replication practices.
- Output: `experiments/risks_and_reproducibility.md`
- Status: completed

8. `WS8 - Final Integrated Research Brief`
- Goal: combine all streams into a full thesis-grade brief.
- Output: `output/final_research_brief.md`
- Status: completed
